Peaceful Plans is a static website. There are no accounts, no sign in, and no forms that send your personal information to us.

## The tools

The readiness checklist, the probate fee calculator, and the documents checklist run entirely in your browser, on your device. Nothing you tick, type, or calculate is sent anywhere, stored anywhere, or shared with anyone. When you close the page, it is gone, which is why the tools offer a print button.

## Analytics

We use privacy respecting analytics to count visits and see which guides help people most. That data is aggregate: pages viewed, approximate region, device type. It does not identify you, and we do not sell it, because there is nothing individual to sell.

## Advertising

If advertising appears on this site in the future, it will be served by Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can control ad personalization in your Google account settings and through your browser. This policy will be updated before any advertising begins.

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page. Last updated October 15, 2026.
